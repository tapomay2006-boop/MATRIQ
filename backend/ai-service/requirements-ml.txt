# The two models this service can run, both optional, both off by default.
#
#   EXTRACTION_ENABLED=true      Qwen2.5-3B + qwen2.5-3b-cpse-lora-v2 (Phase 1)
#   EMBEDDING_PROVIDER=qwen3     Qwen3-Embedding-0.6B (the vector)
#
# Together this is several GB of wheels and, with a GPU, about 2.0 GB of VRAM
# for the 4-bit adapter plus whatever the embedding model needs. The service
# runs, demos and passes every test without any of it: extraction answers 503
# and the embedding provider falls back to the seeded hash, and both say so.
